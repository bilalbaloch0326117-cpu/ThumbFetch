About ThumbFetch

ThumbFetch is a simple, independent browser-based utility that helps people preview and open available thumbnail images for public YouTube video URLs.

How it works

The tool checks common YouTube thumbnail image addresses in your browser. It does not require a user account or a paid API. Image availability and resolution depend on the video.

Independent service

ThumbFetch is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of YouTube or Google. YouTube and related marks belong to their respective owners.

Use images responsibly

Thumbnails may be protected by copyright. You are responsible for obtaining any permission required for reuse.
