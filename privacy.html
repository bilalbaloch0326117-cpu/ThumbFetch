Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This is a starter privacy policy for ThumbFetch. Review and adapt it to your actual hosting, analytics, advertising, and contact setup before publishing.

Information you enter

The thumbnail URL is processed in your browser to identify the YouTube video ID. This static site does not intentionally send the entered URL to a ThumbFetch application server. Your browser contacts YouTube image servers to check and display thumbnails.

Cookies and advertising

This starter version does not include analytics or advertising scripts. If you later add Google AdSense, analytics, or other third-party services, update this policy to explain their use of cookies, identifiers, and data as required by applicable law and provider policies.

Third-party services

Thumbnail images are requested from YouTube's image hosting infrastructure. Their services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details you publish on your Contact page. Replace the placeholder contact page before launch.

Policy changes

Update this page whenever the site's features, service providers, or data practices change.
